## The entry

`main.py` writes one line of plain text to `/output/entry.txt`: a one-star
review of Monday, written as though Monday were a restaurant.

> My waiter was the alarm clock, the special was a 9 a.m. meeting, and Monday charged me for the whole week.

21 words. A real diner's complaint — a waiter, a special, a bill — whose
joke could only be about Monday: the waiter is the alarm clock, the special
is a 9 a.m. meeting, and the bill covers the whole week.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

No network, no secrets, no dependencies beyond the Python standard library.
